Here are some tips and modfications for this recipe:

  • You can substitute the butter with vegetable oil to make it non-dairy
  • If you want to make the brownies feel more like cake then use 10 tablespoons of butter and 3 eggs but no water, all other directions stay the same.
  • For brownies with a softer inside bake for 30 minutes instead and for crunchier brownies bake for 38 minutes
  • For some add in's, mix in 1 cup of chocolate chips or nutes when preparing batter
Ingredients Amount
Melted Butter 1/2 cup / 8 TBSP
Water 2 Tablespoons
eggs 2
King Arthur Gluten-Free Ultimate Fudge Brownie Mix 1 box/packet

Step by step recipe

  1. Set oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit
  2. Grease a 8 inch square pan
  3. In a bowl whisk the butter, water and eggs
  4. Mix in the brownie mix
  5. Spread evenly in the pan
  6. Bake in the oven for 35 minutes
  7. take out and cool for 20 minutes
  8. take out and cut into 9 pieces
  9. Enjoy!

Total preperation time: 1 hour

Serving size: Can make 9-12 brownie pieces when cut up
