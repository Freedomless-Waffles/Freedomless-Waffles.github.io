Here is a basic tutorial for VIM:

First off, what is VIM? Well VIM is a basic text editor that is very old and is based around being able to use the keyboard for everything, because this thing was here before the mouse itself!
To get started with VIM, open the terminal on your device and create a file simply by typing: vim (file name) and clicking enter.
Then when you are in VIM you will see a blank black screen with some numbers on the side, from here you need to know our basic commands to do anything

.
  1. Use either arrow keys or HJKL to move around on the screen.
  2. Click I to enter insert mode, this is how you can write stuff or change stuff
  3. While in insert mode you have to use arrow keys to move around and you use your basic commands when typing like enter to make a new line and also another tip is if your trying to move around fast hold shift while using arrow keys and you skip around lines super quick.
  4. Now when you want to get out of INSERT mode, click escape and click (all at the same time) shift + ;
  5. This will pop up 2 dots at the bottom where you can type W or Write to save everything you wrote on to the file then click enter and do the same thing but this time say q to exit out of VIM (oh no i cant get out of vim, i guess I have to buy a new PC!)
  6. if you want to exit without saving do the same thing but type q! which will force quit
